Research

Probability · Random Structures · Combinatorics

Research Interests

My research interests lie broadly in probability theory, combinatorics and the study of random discrete structures. I am particularly interested in stochastic processes, random graphs and their asymptotic properties.

Current Research

Random Graphs and Spanning Structures

My interests include minimum spanning arborescences (MSA), cycle-rooted spanning forests (CRSF), random mappings and related random structures.

Stochastic Processes

I am interested in loop-contracting random walks (LCRW) and related probabilistic models.

Random Coverings and Fractals

I am also interested in random coverings of fractal sets and their geometric and probabilistic properties.
