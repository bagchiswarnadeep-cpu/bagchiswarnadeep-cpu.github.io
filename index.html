Welcome to my webpage!

I am a mathematician interested in probability theory and related areas of mathematics.

I am currently pursuing Ph.D. in Mathematics at University of Victoria (UVic), British Columbia, Canada, where I have been since September 2023. Before joining UVic, I completed my Master of Science (M.Sc.) in Mathematics at Indian Institute of Technology Gandhinagar (IIT Gandhinagar), India, and my Bachelor of Science (B.Sc.) in Mathematics at Jadavpur University, Kolkata, India.

I am interested in random graphs, combinatorics, stochastic processes, and their asymptotic properties.

My current research focuses on topics such as minimum spanning arborescences (MSA), cycle-rooted spanning forests (CRSF), random coverings of fractals, and loop-contracting random walks (LCRW).

I am always happy to connect with fellow mathematicians, researchers, students, and anyone who shares an interest in mathematics. Feel free to explore my website and get in touch at swarnadeepbagchi@uvic.ca !

I am currently co-organising the Probability and Dynamics Seminar at UVic. If you are interested in giving a talk or would like to know more about the seminar, please feel free to get in touch with me at swarnadeepbagchi@uvic.ca .
