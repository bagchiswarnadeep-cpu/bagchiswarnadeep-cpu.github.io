From ρ to ∂

Follow one sequential CLEB walk on a complete directed graph with independent Exp(1) edge weights.

Orange vertices belong to the highlighted component. A thick orange arrow marks the current choice or cycle. Pale arrows show earlier choices inside a contraction.

The snowball example was chosen to show repeated contractions on 256 vertices. New sample draws a fresh seed. Braces identify the original vertices that currently act as one. The drawing keeps their positions fixed so you can follow which original arrow supplies each exit.
