Music – A Constant Companion

Music has always been an important part of my life. I received several years of training in Indian classical vocal music, which has deeply shaped the way I listen to and appreciate music. Although Indian classical music remains closest to my heart, my musical interests extend across a wide range of genres, including semi-classical, folk, fusion, rock, and Rabindra Sangeet.

I particularly enjoy the interplay of different musical instruments—from the expressive melodies of the piano, guitar, sitar, violin, and flute to the intricate rhythms of the tabla and drums. I am especially fascinated by how composers incorporate classical ragas into film and web-series soundtracks to evoke emotions, create atmospheres, and bring scenes to life.

For me, a beautiful tune is often the beginning of a musical journey. I usually find myself drawn to the melody first, and only gradually become interested in the lyrics. Perhaps that is why meaningful background scores hold a special place in my heart. I love music that can tell a story, convey an emotion, or create an entire world without necessarily relying on words. Some of my favourite background scores are October Theme, Doshhomi Theme, Latika's Theme, and Jessie's Land.

Whenever I get the opportunity, I also enjoy attending live concerts. There is something particularly special about experiencing music being created and performed in front of you—a connection between the musicians, the music, and the audience that a recording can rarely reproduce.
